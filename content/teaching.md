I was a teaching assistant for **CE 311S: Probability and Statistics for Civil Engineers**, a required undergraduate course taken mostly by sophomores and juniors in the Department of Civil, Architectural, and Environmental Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin, taught by [Dr. Stephen D. Boyles](https://sboyles.github.io), in Spring 2025 and Fall 2025. In both semesters, I led weekly lab sections, held weekly office hours, prepared the slides for every lab session, prepared assignment solutions, and assisted with grading. In 2025, I also earned the Engineering Teaching Assistant Certificate from the Cockrell School of Engineering.

Each lab opened with a short review of the week's lecture and a few worked examples. Students then solved worksheet problems in teams of two or three while I moved between the groups to help them work through the concepts, and at the end of the lab I solved the problems on the board. Before each exam, I ran review sessions and held additional office hours.

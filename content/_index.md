I'm currently a graduate student specializing in Transportation Engineering at the Department of Civil, Architectural, and Environmental Engineering, University of Texas at Austin. My research focuses on traffic assignment, multimodal logistics, and transportation network modeling. I'm particularly interested in applying optimization, simulation, and queueing theory to improve the resilience and efficiency of freight systems and urban mobility.

I am also currently a member of the [Transportation Research Board's Standing Committee on Intermodal Freight and Truck Transportation (AT014)](https://www.mytrb.org/OnlineDirectory/Committee/Details/7365). 

## Research interests

My work centers around three interconnected domains: **traffic assignment and network modelling**, **port–hinterland systems**, and **freight and trucking logistics**. Within these areas, I use operations research techniques and data-driven methods to study and improve multimodal freight and logistics networks.

**Primary focus:** Maritime Logistics, Trucking Logistics, Traffic Assignment, Port Operations, Multimodal Freight Networks

**Allied focus:** Operations Research, Queueing Theory, Discrete Event Simulation, Stochastic User Equilibrium, Network Optimization, Data-Driven Methods

I am also a strong advocate of open source research and strive to release large-scale frameworks along with documentation for community use.

## Recent updates

- **Sep 2026.** Our paper on a simulation framework for evaluating port expansion projects is now under review, and MAPS, the port simulation software behind it, is open source. [[Preprint]](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7447899) [[Try out MAPS]](https://github.com/spartalab/port-simulation)

- **May 2026.** Two of our papers on logit-based stochastic user equilibrium are now under review. In the first, we derive error bounds on link flows, link costs, and total system travel time for any feasible solution to the logit-SUE problem. In the second, we analyze the spectrum of the logit mapping to build faster algorithms for logit-SUE. We are also releasing Logit-SUE, a fast solver with several step-size and algorithm options. [[Assignment paper]](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.21843) [[Error bounds paper]](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7677338) [[Try out Logit-SUE]](https://github.com/spartalab/logit-SUE)
